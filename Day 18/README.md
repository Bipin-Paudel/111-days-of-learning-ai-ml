# Day 18: Gaussian Convolution and the Central Limit Theorem

**🚀 Day 18 of the 111 Days of Learning challenge is complete!**

Today I explored why Gaussian curves behave so beautifully under convolution and how that connects to the Central Limit Theorem:

- 📈 **Gaussian Convolution:** How combining two bell curves stays mathematically elegant.
- 🧠 **CLT Fixed Point Intuition:** Why the Gaussian acts like the universal fixed point of repeated averaging.
- 🔄 **Symmetry in 3D Graphs:** How visual symmetry makes the convolution idea easier to understand.
- 💡 **Big Picture:** Why repeated combining of random variables naturally leads to a normal shape.

This made the CLT feel much deeper and more visual. 💡
